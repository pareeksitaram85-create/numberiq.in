ISD control

Reconciled on totals, never invoice-by-invoice.

The 2A ISD sheet holds invoices received by the ISD registration. The book entries are monthly distribution journals carrying no invoice number and no supplier. They are opposite ends of the same mechanism and share no key — an invoice-level match is not possible, and a difference here is not by itself an error.

Out of scope for 2A

Decided by the GST bucket, never by voucher type: no ordinary GST but a special head present. Getting this wrong by excluding "GST Journal" vouchers once produced 23,018 false "In 2A only" rows worth ₹12.78 crore — 95.2% of those journals genuinely are in 2A.

ItemRowsTaxMeaning

Cross-year timing and s.16(4)

ITC for a financial year cannot be taken after 30 November following the end of that year, or the furnishing of the annual return, whichever is earlier.

The 30 November limb is what is computed here, because it is the one that binds in practice. If the annual return for the year has already been filed, the real deadline is earlier than the date shown and these rows must be read against that date instead.

s.17(5) blocked credits by clause

Screened on narration and voucher type only — never on supplier name. Deliberately conservative: it is a prompt to look, not a determination.

ClauseDescriptionRowsTax
Why "gift" alone is never enough. s.17(5)(h) blocks goods disposed of by way of gift or free sample. A bare keyword once flagged 240 rows worth ₹42.7 lakh that read "purchase of gift items" and "gift box 860 Pcs" — gift boxes bought to sell. The gift limb is therefore scoped to disposal wording and skipped on Purchase vouchers, and clause (b)(i) is skipped on Purchase vouchers too because the proviso to (b) restores the credit where inward food makes an outward taxable supply of the same category.

Second proviso to s.16(2) — the 180-day rule

Where the supplier is not paid within 180 days of the invoice date, the credit is added back to output tax with interest, and may be re-availed on payment.
